⚠ System & Font Compatibility:
Characters render according to the fonts installed on your Operating System (Windows 10/11, macOS, Linux, ChromeOS).
If some rare characters (such as CJK, Tibetan, or Runic) display empty boxes, you can download these free fonts:

• Google Noto Fonts http://fonts.google.com/noto
• BabelStone Fonts http://www.babelstone.co.uk/Fonts/
• Quivira http://www.quivira-font.com/
• DejaVu Fonts http://dejavu-fonts.org/
